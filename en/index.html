About me

Hi, I'm Jonathan.

I've spent over a decade in operations, and it's always the same story: the problems that cost the most (sales that slip away, inventory that doesn't add up, reports that arrive late) are rarely bad luck. They almost always come from tasks that are still done by hand.

I come from the operations side, not just the technical one. I managed logistics, inventory and finance at a distributor for thirteen years, so I understand the problem from the inside: I don't just spot the bottleneck, I know how to build the fix.

Today I combine that operational base with automation and applied AI, mainly with Claude Code, so processes stop depending on manual work.
