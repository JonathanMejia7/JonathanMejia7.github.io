Keep AI from training on your business data

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini can now connect to your business. On personal accounts, free or paid, what you share with them can be used to improve their models. Here is the setting to turn that off in each one. It takes two minutes.

  • Guide
  • ChatGPT · Claude · Gemini

ChatGPT

  • Mobile app
  • Web
  • Desktop app

You only do it once: the setting applies to your account on your phone, on the web and in the desktop app.

  1. Open Settings

    In the mobile app: open the sidebar and tap your profile. On the web or in the desktop app: open your account menu.

  2. Go to Data controls
  3. Turn off “Improve the model for everyone”

    On the web and in the desktop app, tap Done to save.

  • Your chats are not deleted: they stay in your history.
  • If you give a response a thumbs up or down, that whole conversation can still be used for training.
  • Voice mode has its own control.
  • Another option: temporary chat is not used for training. It is kept for up to 30 days for safety.

Claude

  • Mobile app
  • Web
  • Desktop app

The steps are the same on your phone, on the web and in the desktop app. It is an account setting. On the web you can also go straight to claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls

  1. Tap your name and open Settings
  2. Go to Privacy
  3. Under Preferences, turn off “Help improve our AI models”
  • Your saved chats stop being used in future training. Training that already happened is not undone.
  • If you give a thumbs up or down, that conversation is kept for up to 5 years and can be used for training.
  • Incognito chats are not used for training.
  • What Claude reads from your connectors, like Google Drive, is not used for training unless you copy it into the chat.

Gemini

  • Mobile app
  • Web

You only do it once: it is a setting on your Google account.

  1. Open your Gemini activity

    In the mobile app: tap the menu, then your profile picture and Gemini Apps Activity. On the web: at gemini.google.com, open Settings & help and go to Activity. You can also go straight to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini

  2. Tap “On” at the top

    The setting is called Keep Activity and it is on by default.

  3. Choose Turn off

    Or “Turn off and delete activity” if you also want to delete past activity.

  • With it off, your new chats are not used for training. Google still keeps them for up to 72 hours to respond to you.
  • Some chats are reviewed by people, and those are kept for up to 3 years even if you delete your activity. The same goes for feedback you send.
  • Temporary chats are not used for training.

Menu names are shown as they appear in English. Checked on November 9 and 15, 2026, against the official help pages of OpenAI, Anthropic and Google.

Keep in mind

If you use a business account

ChatGPT Business or Enterprise and Claude’s business plans do not train on your data by default. In Gemini with a Google Workspace account, your administrator controls the setting.

Before you upload any file

Turning off training does not mean the tool doesn’t read your data: it reads it to answer you. Before uploading anything with customers, balances or salaries, remove whatever the question doesn’t need.
